An original spot-the-difference game, drawn entirely in code: two near-identical
pictures side by side, seven hidden differences per round, a ticking clock and a
score to chase. Round one watches a harbor at dusk; round two wakes a garden at
morning. Play with mouse or keyboard, ask for a hint when you are stuck, and
find them all before the light runs out.

Play it: serve `game/` with any static server and open `index.html`.
